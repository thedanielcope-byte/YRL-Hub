Standard Operating Procedure

How to Log In to the Agent Hub

A step-by-step guide for Your Realty Link agents to access the Agent Hub at yrl.nomadsystems.co

Back to Agent Hub

Before You Begin

The Agent Hub uses a passwordless sign-in system. Instead of a password, you'll receive a one-time 6-digit code sent to your email each time you sign in. This is more secure and means you never have to remember a password.

  • You must be an authorized agent to access the hub. Your broker adds you to the access list.
  • You'll need access to the email address on file with Your Realty Link.
  • Codes expire after 1 hour, so enter it promptly after receiving it.
1

Go to the Agent Hub Login Page

Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, etc.) and go to:

yrl.nomadsystems.co

You'll see the YRL Agent Hub login screen with the Your Realty Link logo and an email input field.

The Agent Hub login page at yrl.nomadsystems.co
2

Enter Your Work Email

Type your work email address into the "Work Email" field. This must be the email address that your broker has authorized for hub access (usually your @yourrealtylink.com email).

Then click the "Email Me a Code" button.

Enter your authorized work email and click "Email Me a Code"
If you see an "Access Denied" message, your email may not be authorized yet. Contact Janet Giles-Schultz at 317-997-7404 or janet@yourrealtylink.com to get set up.
3

Check Your Email for the Code

Within a few seconds, you'll receive an email with your 6-digit sign-in code. Open your email inbox and look for a message with the subject line containing your code number.

The email you'll receive with your 6-digit sign-in code
Don't see the email? Check your junk/spam folder. The email comes from Nomad Systems Co. If it's not there after a minute, click "Send a new code" on the login page to get a fresh one.
4

Enter the Code and Sign In

Go back to the login page in your browser. You should now see the "Enter Your Code" screen. Type in the 6-digit code from your email, then click "Sign In".

Type in your 6-digit code and click "Sign In"
If the code doesn't work, it may have expired (codes last 1 hour). Click "Send a new code" to get a fresh one. You can also click "Use a different email" if you entered the wrong address.
5

You're In — Welcome to the Agent Hub

After entering the correct code, you'll be automatically redirected to the Agent Hub dashboard — Your Command Center. From here you can access all your tools, resources, and assets.

The Agent Hub dashboard — your command center for everything YRL

Your session stays active so you won't need to sign in every time you visit. If you're ever signed out, just repeat these steps.

Troubleshooting

Q: I never received the code email.
Check your spam/junk folder first. The email comes from "Nomad Systems Co." If it's not there, wait 60 seconds and click "Send a new code." If you still don't receive it, contact your broker — your email may need to be added to the authorized agents list.
Q: I got an "Access Denied" or "not authorized" message.
Your email hasn't been added to the hub's access list yet. Contact Janet Giles-Schultz at 317-997-7404 or janet@yourrealtylink.com to get authorized.
Q: My code isn't working.
Codes expire after 1 hour. Make sure you're entering the most recent code (check for newer emails). Click "Send a new code" to get a fresh one. Also make sure you're typing all 6 digits correctly.
Q: I was signed in but now it says my access was revoked.
Your hub access has been removed by your broker. If you believe this is a mistake, contact Janet Giles-Schultz directly.
Q: How do I sign out?
On the Agent Hub dashboard, click "Sign Out" in the top navigation bar. You'll be returned to the login page.
