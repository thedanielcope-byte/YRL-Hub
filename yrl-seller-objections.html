The Isolate Technique

For every objection, start the same way: Isolate the objection. Ask: "Other than [their concern], is there any other reason you wouldn't list with me today?" This separates real objections from stalling and focuses the conversation on solving one specific concern.

"I have other agents to interview"

Isolate First

"Other than wanting to interview other agents, is there any other reason you wouldn't list with me today?"

Response
At this point in our conversation, when a seller says they would list with me and still goes through with additional appointments, it's usually because they feel obligated to have the other agent over. Is that the case here?

Let me save you time as well as start the process tomorrow to find you a buyer. After all, while you are waiting to be polite, there may be a buyer out there that needs to buy tomorrow. We want them to know about your house, right?

Let me do this — I don't mind. I've done it for many of my sellers. I'll call the other agents and tell them that you liked them and were looking forward to meeting with them, but you decided to list with me. And if they have a buyer, we would love for them to bring them by. And of course if they buy the house, I'll naturally pay them part of my commission.

"We've never heard of your company"

Isolate First

"Other than not having heard of us, is there any other reason you wouldn't list with me today?"

Response
I can appreciate that. In fact, thank you for bringing that up. As we think about who really sells a house, it is the agent, not the company, right?

At Your Realty Link, every agent on our team wants to help get your house sold. We work together, not against each other. At other companies I've seen, agents competed against each other for recognition. There is no financial benefit if one agent sells a house and another doesn't.

Wouldn't you rather have an agent that has the support of the entire team and all the agents helping to sell your house, instead of a company where the agent is on their own to sell it?

"The other agent would do it for less commission"

Isolate First

"Other than the commission, is there any other reason you wouldn't list with me today?"

Response A
So what I hear you saying is you want to net the most money possible, right? Every seller that decides to choose me wants the same thing. See, many people think that they will get the same price regardless of who is conducting the negotiations, which we already know isn't true — otherwise top companies wouldn't pay hundreds of thousands of dollars to hire the best negotiators.

The other agent has already shown you their negotiation skills by giving up their own money. What will happen when you ask them to take care of your money? Won't they give it away faster than they gave their own money away?

I think you prefer a bulldog like me that will watch your money and negotiate on your behalf. Right?
Response B
I understand your hope to save money, and it sounds like you are interested in putting the most money in your pocket — is that right?

See, the MLS shows the average agent in your city is negotiating about 95% of the list price. The same MLS shows me at over 99%. So you can see that I get sellers more money than the average agent. So if you are interested in placing more money in your pocket, then hire me to negotiate for you.

Since you intend to get the most money, let's sign the listing agreement and get you moving to where you want to be.
Response C
Has there ever been a time in your life when you paid less for something and found out that you got what you paid for? This time is like that — may I explain?

See, many agents take a lesser commission because they haven't been taught negotiating skills. You are placing one of your most expensive possessions into the hands of someone that has proven to you that the best sales tactic they know how to do is to lower the price. And if they lower their price to sell your house, then you won't be saving any money — in fact you could lose money.

I know if you are like my other sellers, you will choose a bulldog like me that will watch your money as much as I watch mine.

"We want to think it over"

Isolate First

"Other than wanting to think it over, is there any other reason you wouldn't list with me today?"

Response A
Great. It is important that you make the right decision. What specifically do you need to think about?
If they mention another agent, switch to the "other agents" handler. If it's price, go to the pricing handler.
Response B
I can appreciate that. This is a pretty important decision you're about to make to sell your house. This is what other people have had me do and I'm more than happy to do it for you.

Let's fill out the paperwork and then you can think it over. When you choose to hire me, you can give me a call. That way if you choose to hire me, I'll come by and pick up the paperwork and I can get your house on the market in minutes.
Fill out the paperwork, then say: "If you feel comfortable, you can allow me to take the paperwork and when you call me tomorrow, if you say you've decided not to list with me, I will rip up the contract and mail it to you. If you decide to list with me, then I can get the sign up and the marketing started immediately. Doesn't that make sense?"

"Another agent said they could get me more money"

Isolate First

"Other than that, is there any other reason you wouldn't list with me today?"

Response
Wow. Are they going to buy the house? The reason I ask is in today's market the buyer determines the price they want to offer and you determine if you'll accept it.

I could put your house on the market at the price the other agent is willing to, and you know what scares me about that? You would have the same problem that you'll have with any agent. Agents in our area know what a house is worth, and when they have a buyer, they sit down at the computer and find houses in the price range their buyer is willing to pay. They show the houses that are the best value.

Based on what I showed you this evening, does it make sense that they would show your house with a higher price than the others? Of course they would — if they wanted to sell another house they had listed, they would use your house to convince the buyers they were getting a better deal on the other house. In essence, you would help the other house look like a great value.

So the question is: Do you want to help sell other houses?
If they still want a higher price
I can understand what you want. Let me help you out. Since the other agent says they can get a higher price, why don't you ask them to put in their contract that if they ask for a price reduction or bring you a lower offer, then you can cancel the contract. See how quickly they start talking a different language. I mean, after all, if they can get you that price, then they should be willing to put that stipulation in the contract, right?

"Your price is too low / I think it's worth more"

Phrase 1: The Market Interpreter
I don't make the market — I only interpret it. The market is telling us we need to price it right. I'm the professional, like a doctor, and I am telling you the market shows we need to price it right. Otherwise you're going to have a terrible disease called "no showings."
Phrase 2: The Reality Check
The National Association of Realtors tracks statistics for us, and they state that ten showings, no offers, or two weeks without a showing means the house is overpriced.
Phrase 3: The Auction Effect
Have you ever been to an auction? It's amazing to watch the bidding go up and up when more than one person is interested. Well, by pricing the property right, you can create that same auction effect. When you price yours to be the best value, you may have more than one buyer interested, creating an auction effect that drives the price up and gets you more money. You want to have the most money, right?
Phrase 4: The Stock Analogy
If you bought stock last year and that stock was $49 per share, and today you want to sell that same stock and it is worth $25 per share, what could we sell it at? $25, right? I don't make the market, I interpret it, and the market is telling us we need to sell at market price.
Phrase 5: The Messenger
Don't kill the messenger. I am only delivering the message the market is stating. Anytime you feel uncomfortable, then you know I am telling you the truth. You do want your agent to be honest, don't you?
