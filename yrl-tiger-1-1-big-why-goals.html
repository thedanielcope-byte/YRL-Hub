YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 1.1: Your Big Why & Goals

01Why this module matters

Real estate hands you total freedom — no one sets your schedule, your goals, or your paycheck. That freedom is also the trap. The agents who make it aren't the smartest or the most charming; they're the ones who know why they're doing this and exactly what they're aiming for, so they lead-generate even when they don't feel like it.

This is the first module for a reason. Everything else in Tiger Training — scripts, listings, buyers, negotiation — is just how. Today you lock in your why and your what.

04Break it into scoreboard numbers

05Start your database — people who already know you

Your first deals come from your sphere. List everyone who'd take your call: family, friends, past coworkers, your dentist, your kid's coach. Add as many as you can — aim for 20+.

Next step: in Module 2.1 we'll load these into your database and start a simple monthly touch plan. For now, just get the names down.
