Closing Scripts

9 proven approaches to get the listing agreement signed

Seller Tools

Getting to the Signature

After you've presented your marketing plan, discussed pricing, and shown where buyers come from, it's time to ask for the business. Choose the closing approach that fits the moment. You don't need to use all nine — pick the one that matches the conversation and the seller's personality.

1

The Olympian Close

Best for: Confident sellers

Many people think all real estate agents are the same. Let me ask you — if one of you had to run to your mailbox, could you? Sure, most people could. Yet would you go to the Olympics for running? Of course not.

See, just because you can run doesn't make you an Olympian, and just because you have a real estate license doesn't mean you can get the job done.

Go ahead and hire me. I'm the Olympian compared to most agents, and I know you want someone that can sell your house — don't you?
2

The Expert Close

Best for: Analytical sellers

If you were in need of surgery and you were going to hire a doctor to perform the surgery, would you hire someone that had performed hundreds of surgeries successfully, or someone who was just starting out?

Why? That's why so many people hire me to sell their house.
3

The Confidence Close

Best for: Ready-to-go sellers

Do you think I can sell your house? Great — put me to work, and let me show you how I get houses sold.
Short and direct. Use when the seller has been engaged and positive throughout the presentation.
4

The Value Close

Best for: Price-focused sellers

Any agent can list your house — and yet it's like your job. Do you think you do a great job? What if you knew you could do the job and your boss wanted to hire someone at a cheaper rate that didn't have a track record? What if he gave them your job?

Would it make sense for him to lose money? Training the new person and allowing them to learn the job you already know means you could do it faster, therefore saving him money.

So sometimes hiring someone for less actually costs you more, right?
5

The Direct Close

Best for: Decisive sellers

Go ahead and put me to work for you tonight.
The simplest and often most effective close. Hand them the pen with the listing agreement. Silence after this line is your friend — let them respond.
6

The Assurance Close

Best for: Nervous sellers

You'll always be glad you took action. I will make certain all the agents know about your house, as well as calling and asking if they have a buyer for your house.
7

The No-Pressure Close

Best for: Hesitant sellers

I'm not here to pressure you into making a bad decision. I am here making certain you have enough information to make a great decision.
Follow up with a gentle pause, then: "Based on everything we've talked about tonight, are you ready to get started?"
8

The Team Advantage Close

Best for: Sellers comparing agents

Why put one agent to work when you can get a whole team for the same price? At Your Realty Link, when you hire me, you get every agent on our team working to find a buyer for your home. That's more connections, more showings, and more opportunities to sell.
9

The Dream Close

Best for: Emotionally motivated sellers

So, all we need to do now is sign the agreement so you can get to [where they want to go] by [when they want to be there]. Let's make that happen.
This is the most powerful close because it connects the signature directly to their personal motivation. Reference back to what they told you during the pre-qualifying call or at the beginning of the presentation. Use their own words.

 Pro Tips

  • After you deliver a closing line, be quiet. The first person who speaks loses.
  • Place the listing agreement and pen in front of the seller as you deliver the close.
  • If they object, use the Objection Handlers and then close again with a different script.
  • Always tie back to their motivation — the "why" they told you at the beginning.
  • It's not about being pushy. It's about being confident enough to ask for the business.
