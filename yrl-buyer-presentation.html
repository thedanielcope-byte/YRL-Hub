The Buyer Compensation Agreement
As of August 2024, all buyers must sign a written agreement before touring homes. Here's what that means for you.

What Is It?

A written agreement between you and your agent that outlines the services you'll receive and how your agent is compensated. It's now required by Indiana law before we can show you any homes.

Why Does It Exist?

The real estate industry changed its rules to make agent compensation more transparent. This protects you by ensuring you know exactly what services you're getting and what they cost — before you start looking.

How Does It Work?

Three simple steps:

  • 1
    We agree on the services I'll provide and my compensation
  • 2
    We search for homes together — I represent YOUR interests
  • 3
    At closing, seller concessions often cover the cost — but you're protected either way
Your Realty Link agents earn their compensation. We negotiate on your behalf, protect your interests, and guide you through one of the biggest financial decisions of your life.
