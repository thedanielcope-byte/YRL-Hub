YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 4.2: Your Three Key Hires

01Only three people sit within arm's reach

The marketing & administrative manager runs lead generation execution, communication systems, financial systems, and the rest of the back office. The lead buyer specialist secures appointments, signs representation agreements, shows and sells. The lead listing specialist secures appointments, takes listings, runs seller communication and negotiates offers.

Everyone else reports to one of those three. Get those three right and the rest of the organisation becomes their job rather than yours.

The order matters and almost everybody gets it wrong. Administrative help comes first — not a buyer agent. Salespeople are generally poor at building systems, so hiring sales first means hiring someone unsuited to half of what the role actually requires.

The order of hires is not a preference — it is a sequence.

03Write the job before you write the ad

A detailed job description is a tool, not paperwork. It sets the standard on the front end, tells the person how they succeed and how they fail, and gives you something objective to hold them accountable to later. Think of it as a syllabus.

04Hire for five years

Ask for a five-year commitment from each of these three. They should see the role as potentially their life's work, not a stop. No matter how good the candidate, don't settle for someone who can't convincingly show that.

And remember that big goals are recruiting magnets. Talent is attracted to a vision of the future and the challenge of getting there. Modest goals and a thin job description attract people who want a job that won't stretch them — and then your business won't stretch either.

Two standing hours a week is the whole management load: one hour with the person who runs the business, one with all three together. The agenda is the goals. There is no other agenda.
