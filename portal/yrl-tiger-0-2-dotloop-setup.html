YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 0.2: Setting Up Dotloop

01What Dotloop is

Dotloop is where every transaction lives — you prepare documents, get clients to sign electronically, and store the complete file. Your Realty Link pays for your Dotloop, but you need a few things in place first.

02Get set up

YRL pays for your Dotloop, but you must have your license number and MIBOR membership before admin can set you up. While you wait, another agent can share their login so you can start learning — confirm with Janet.

03Learn the basics

Once you're in, get comfortable creating a file (titled by property address), pulling documents from templates, and sending them for signature. You'll use this on every listing and every offer — the workflow modules (4.4, 3.3) walk through the documents themselves.
