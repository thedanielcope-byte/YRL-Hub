YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 2.5: Online & Social Media Leads

01Why show up online

When someone in your circle thinks 'real estate,' you want your face to appear. Consistent, genuine posting keeps you top-of-mind and quietly proves you're active and knowledgeable — long before anyone reaches out.

You don't need to go viral. You need to be visible and human to the few hundred people who already know you.

02What to post

Mix these so you're helpful, local, and human — not a walking billboard.

Tie into Your Realty Link's channels — post your open houses to the YRL Agent Talk page, and use the YouTube channel (YourRealtyLinkIndy) content where it helps.

03Turn engagement into conversations

Likes don't close deals — conversations do. When someone engages, take it to a message. Be a helpful human, not a salesperson.

DM follow-up script

You: Hey [Name], thanks for the like/comment! Are you all set where you are, or is a move somewhere on the horizon?

(If curious → answer helpfully, offer a market value or a few listings, and add them to your database.)
