Buyer Wishlist Property Feature Sheet

Using these

  • Fair housing — the wishlist. Write down what the buyer tells you they want. Do not suggest or steer toward areas based on who lives there, and don't answer "what's the neighborhood like?" with anything about people. Point them at public data — schools, crime statistics, census — and let them draw their own conclusions.
  • Schools. Record the district the buyer names. Don't rate schools or advise which are "good" — direct them to the Indiana Department of Education and the school corporation.
  • The feature sheet is marketing, not a disclosure. Everything on it must be accurate and verifiable. Square footage, year built, taxes and school assignment come from public record or the seller's disclosure — never from memory or a guess.
  • Never state a commission on a feature sheet or anywhere else.
  • Print at 100% scale. Both sheets are designed to fit one page each.
