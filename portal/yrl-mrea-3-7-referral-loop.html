YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 3.7: The Referral Reward Loop

01Educate, ask, reward

Educate. Every contact reminds people that you sell real estate, that you're good at it, and that you prefer to work by referral. Most of your sphere genuinely does not know that last part.

Ask for help. Directly. “If you know anyone thinking about buying or selling, please share their name with me or my name with them.” Not implied — asked.

Reward. Immediately, and for the referral itself. This is the part almost everybody gets wrong.

Treat the referral source better than the referral. They took a risk on their own reputation to send you a friend. That deserves more attention than the transaction does.

02Four touchpoints, not one

Design what happens at each. Small and immediate beats large and eventual.

Check what your brokerage and Indiana licensing rules allow before offering anything of value for a referral, particularly to anyone who holds a licence or works in a related industry. Confirm with Janet Giles-Schultz (317-997-7404) before you publish a reward.

03Your allied resources

The people who should be sending you business every single year — and receiving it from you.

This is a small, deliberate group: lenders, title, inspectors, contractors from the vendor directory, agents in other markets, and past clients who have already referred once. They get everything your Met database gets, plus one-to-one contact several times a year. The most influential, as often as monthly.
