YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 3.2: The 8×8 Builder

01Why eight, and why eight weeks

Buyers and sellers hold room for almost no agents at all. Research on how people choose an agent shows most contact only one, and a further chunk contact two. You are first, second, or you are not in the conversation.

Eight touches across eight consecutive weeks is the observed minimum to form that bond. Not four. Not three. Anything shorter and the name fades before the need arrives — which in real estate can be a year later.

If your current follow-up is a three-email sequence, this module is the single highest-value change on the whole path. Same leads, same spend, more of them remembering you.

03The two calls

These are the touches that do the work. Automate the reminder, never the call.

Week 4 call

Hi ___, it's ___ with Your Realty Link. Did I catch you at a bad time?

Did you happen to receive the market update I sent over? Have you had a chance to look at it?

The reason I'm calling — I'm just checking in to see if you happen to know of anyone who might be buying or selling a home right now.

Week 8 call

Hi ___, it's ___ with Your Realty Link. Did I catch you at a bad time?

Did you get the ___ I sent you? Any questions come up from it?

I'll keep sending you things I think are genuinely useful. I'd love to be your Realtor for life — and if you know anyone thinking about buying or selling, please pass my name along or send theirs to me.

Always send something physical before a call. It gives you an opening that isn't “just checking in” — and “did you get the ___ I sent?” is a far easier sentence to say.
