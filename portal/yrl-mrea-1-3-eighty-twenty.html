YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 1.3: The 80/20 Audit

01Activity is not productivity

You can be genuinely busy and genuinely unproductive at the same time — the two feel identical from the inside. The only way to tell them apart is to write the week down and grade it.

In this business the 20% is short and boring: generating leads, going on listing appointments, marketing listings, and building leverage. Nearly everything else — however necessary — is the 80%.

The 80% is not worthless. Contracts have to be written and files have to close. The question is only whether you should be the one doing it, and whether it happens before or after your 20%.
