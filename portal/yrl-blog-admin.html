How to publish a blog post
  1. Click New (left) to start a post.
  2. Type a Title — the web address (slug) fills in automatically.
  3. Pick a Category and upload a Cover image.
  4. Write a short Summary (shows in the blog list & on Google).
  5. Write the Post body — add headings, links, and images with the toolbar.
  6. (Optional) add FAQ questions for SEO.
  7. Set the Author and Publish date/time — a future time schedules it.
  8. Click Publish (or Save draft to finish later).

Goes live at yourrealtylink.com/blog/ automatically within ~15 minutes (or at your scheduled time). Drafts stay private.
