YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 2.3: The Budget Model

01Lead with revenue

One discipline underpins the whole model: make the money before you spend it. Real estate is unusually kind here — the cost of acquiring one listing is low relative to what it returns, so the business can fund its own growth if you let it.

Second: stop thinking of expenses as costs and start thinking of them as investments that owe you a return. A dollar in should bring back several. If a dollar returns a dollar, keep the dollar and save yourself the work.

Percentages hold remarkably steady as a business grows, even though the dollar amounts change completely. That's why this is a model and not a one-time budget.
