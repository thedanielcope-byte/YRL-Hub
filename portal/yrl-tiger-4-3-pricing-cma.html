YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 4.3: Pricing Right & Presenting the CMA

01Why pricing is everything

Overpriced homes sit, go stale, and sell for less than if they'd been priced right from day one. Your job is to protect the seller from their own optimism with data — and to price for how buyers actually shop.

The first two weeks on market get the most attention. Price right out of the gate and you capture that surge instead of chasing the market down.

02Build the CMA

Comparable sales tell the real story. Use what you can pull from the BLC and public records.

The CMA tool in ConnectBLC is limited — pull what you can and fill gaps from comps and tax records. Confirm the current process with Janet.

03Present the price with confidence

Lead with data, then handle the inevitable pushback.

'But Zillow says it's worth more' script

You: I hear you — those online estimates are a starting point, but they can't walk your home or account for the recent sales right around you the way I can.

Here's what actually sold nearby and what's competing with you today. Based on the real numbers, here's where I'd price it to sell — and why.
