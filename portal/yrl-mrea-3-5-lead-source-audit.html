YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 3.5: Lead Source Audit

01You cannot manage what you don't source

Every lead has to be captured (who, and how do we reach them), sourced (where did it come from), assigned (who owns the follow-up) and stored (where do we find it again). Miss the sourcing step and you are spending blind forever.

The reward for tracking is that you stop arguing about marketing and start reading it. Ranked cost-per-closing turns a matter of opinion into a matter of record.

Give any new source six to twelve months before you judge it. Marketing response is back-loaded — you are waiting for people to need you, and that timing is theirs, not yours.
