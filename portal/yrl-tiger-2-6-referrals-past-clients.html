YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 2.6: Referrals & Past-Client Follow-Up

01Why referrals are the endgame

Referred clients are easier to work with, close more often, and cost nothing to acquire. The catch: referrals don't happen by luck — they happen because you stayed in touch and made it easy to send you people.

Most agents lose touch the day after closing. Staying in your clients' lives is the single highest-ROI habit in real estate.

02Stay in their lives after closing

03Ask for the referral (the right way)

Make it specific and easy. 'Anyone?' gets nothing; a clear ask gets names.

Referral ask script

You: [Name], it was such a pleasure helping you. I build my business on referrals from people like you rather than spending on ads.

Who's the next person you know who might be thinking about a move — a coworker, neighbor, family member? I'd take great care of them.

(Get the name, ask for a warm intro, and follow up.)

04Reconnect with past clients

Start now — even clients from a past life or job. List who you'll call back.
