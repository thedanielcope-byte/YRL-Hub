YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 4.4: List a Home for Sale

01Before you meet — research the property

Walk in knowing more about the home than the seller expects. In ConnectBLC, search the address for any old listing — you can reuse the old BLC number later to speed up creating the new listing, and pull comps for a partial CMA.

These are Your Realty Link's current steps. Systems and forms change — confirm anything you're unsure about with Janet Giles-Schultz (317-997-7404).

02Prepare your listing documents in Dotloop

Dotloop is our property & document management system. Set the file up before you meet.

Create a new Dotloop file titled by the street address. Get each signer's email early (every seller who must sign needs their own email). Pull your first set of listing documents from the templates and fill them in.

Open a new Dotloop file for every property by street address. Dotloop has no delete — archive, don't delete. Confirm the current template set and net-sheet form with Janet.

04Who signs the listing contract

Anyone on the tax records / deed as an owner (or a legal power-of-attorney designee) must sign. Special cases: in a divorce, both parties sign; on a death of an owner, you'll need a death certificate for the title company and the executor signs — order preliminary title work early and find out if the sale needs court approval.

Your principal broker signs and accepts every listing. Add the broker to the Dotloop file with edit and signing rights.

05Photos

Great pictures sell houses. Take at least one exterior and one interior on your phone at the appointment so you can post to social immediately. For the real listing, use a professional if you can — YRL has photographers (roughly $150–250 per listing).

06Post the home on the BLC

Reuse the old BLC number and the tax records to speed up entering the new listing — you'll need very little other info. The listing must be online within 2 days of any advertising (sign, social post, etc.).

BrokerBay is MIBOR's approved showing vendor and is included in tech fees. Confirm the current listing-agent/co-agent setup and any fees with Janet before you enter a listing on your own.
