YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 1.3: Business Planning Clinic

01Why plan at all

Nobody hands a real estate agent a schedule or a quota. That freedom is why most agents drift — and why the ones with a written plan run circles around them. Your plan doesn't need to be fancy. It needs to fit on one page and tell you what to do this week.

The whole game reduces to one chain: conversations → appointments → signed agreements → closings. Plan the front of that chain and the back takes care of itself.
