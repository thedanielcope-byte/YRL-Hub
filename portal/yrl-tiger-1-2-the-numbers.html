01Why this matters

Real estate pays in lump sums, weeks or months apart, with nobody withholding your taxes. Agents who don't understand their numbers spend a $9,000 commission like it's $9,000 — then panic at tax time. Agents who do understand them build a real business.

You are a 1099 independent contractor, not an employee. No taxes are withheld. What lands in your account is not what you get to keep.

02How a commission actually flows

A home sells. The total commission (agreed in the listing) is split between the listing side and the buying side. Your side's commission goes to your brokerage first — then you get your split of it, minus any fees.

So one closing runs: Sale price → total commission → your side's share → your split with Your Realty Link → minus fees → minus what you set aside for taxes → your take-home.
