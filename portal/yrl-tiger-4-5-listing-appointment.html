YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 4.5: The Listing Appointment

01The appointment is a presentation

You're not just chatting — you're presenting a plan to sell their biggest asset. Because you sent the prelisting packet, they already trust Your Realty Link. Now walk the home, present your price and marketing plan, and ask for the business.

02Walk the home & build rapport

03Present price & plan, then ask

Don't hint — ask for the signature directly.

The close

You: Based on everything we've covered — the comps, your timeline, and our marketing plan — I recommend we list at [price].

I'd love to get to work for you. Let's get the paperwork started so we can have this live and in front of buyers this week. Sound good?

(Then walk them through signing in Dotloop or on paper.)

04Handle 'we want to think about it'

Objection script

You: Absolutely — this is a big decision. What specifically would you like to think over? Is it the price, the plan, or something else?

(Address the real concern. Often it's one question away from a yes.)
