YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 1.1: The Three L's Diagnostic

01The whole business on one page

After interviewing the highest-producing agents in the country, the authors of The Millionaire Real Estate Agent tried to group what separated them. They started with eight factors. They got it down to five. In the end it came down to three.

Leads. You have two jobs — real estate, and generating clients. You can be the best-informed agent in Marion County and it earns you nothing without people to serve. Until you have more leads than your goals require, nothing else is the problem.

Listings. Seller listings are the high-leverage, maximum-earning side of this business. They take less time per dollar than buyers, they give you control of your schedule, and — the part most agents miss — a listing is a marketing platform. A properly marketed listing should produce about one closed buyer on its own.

Leverage. Who does it (people), how they do it (systems), what they do it with (tools). When you have gone as far as your own hours can take you, leverage is the only remaining move.

The 80/20 rule is doing the work here. Leads, Listings, and Leverage are the 20% of your activity that produces 80% of your result. Everything else is real, but it is not the lever.

04The one thing

The temptation now is to fix all three. Don't. The order is fixed for a reason — leads before listings, listings before leverage. You cannot leverage a business that has no leads to work, and hiring into a lead problem just means paying someone to be idle.
