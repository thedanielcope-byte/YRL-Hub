01The trade you're making

For every 50 people you mail 12 times a year, expect about 1 closing. Worse than the 12:2 you get from your sphere — but you can run it against five thousand people without adding an hour to your week.

That is the whole reason it matters. Sphere marketing is limited by your personal capacity. Farm marketing is limited only by budget. An agent who wants to eventually step back from daily selling needs the second kind.

Pick the farm where Your Realty Link is already strong online — the city pages with real maps, curated community events and local blog coverage. Mail plus a deep local page beats mail alone. Don't scatter across six towns.
