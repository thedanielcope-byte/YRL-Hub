YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 5.2: Getting Reviews & Referrals

01Why reviews matter

Before someone hires you, they check what others say. A steady stream of genuine 5-star reviews on Google and Facebook makes every future lead easier to win. The best time to ask is right after closing, when your client is thrilled.

02Ask for the review

Review ask

You: I'm so happy for you! One quick favor — reviews are how people find me. Would you mind leaving a few words about your experience on Google? I'll text you the link right now so it's easy.

(Send the direct review link immediately, while you're together.)

03Turn happy clients into referrals

A review is public proof; a referral is a warm lead. Pair them: ask for the review, then ask who they know that's thinking about a move (the referral scripts live in Module 2.6).
