YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 2.1: Your Database & Sphere of Influence

01Why the sphere comes first

New agents burn money chasing strangers while a goldmine sits in their phone. The people who already know you will give you your first deals and your first referrals — if they know you're in real estate and hear from you regularly.

A database isn't a list you make once. It's a relationship you work. The goal: everyone in it knows you sell real estate, and no one forgets it.

02Build your list

Add everyone who'd take your call — family, friends, past coworkers, neighbors, your dentist, your kid's coach. Aim for 50+.

Pull from your phone contacts, Facebook friends, holiday card list, and email. Most agents are shocked they can list 100+ people once they start.

03The 'I'm in real estate now' call

The one conversation that starts your business. Read it, then make it yours below.

Sphere announcement script

You: Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] — do you have a quick minute? (wait)

I wanted you to hear it from me: I've joined Your Realty Link and I'm helping people buy and sell homes here in Central Indiana.

I'm not calling to sell you anything — I just want the people I care about to know what I do. If you or anyone you know is ever thinking about a move, I'd love to be your go-to.

You: Out of curiosity — do you know anyone right now who's talked about buying or selling? (listen)

04Stay in touch — the monthly touch plan

Out of sight, out of mind. A simple, consistent touch plan keeps you top-of-mind so you get the call when someone's ready. Mix value and personal — not just 'list with me.'
