YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 2.2: Open Houses That Generate Leads

01Why open houses work

Open houses put you face-to-face with motivated buyers who aren't yet working with an agent — and with neighbors who are curious what their home is worth. Your job isn't to sell the house; it's to start relationships and capture contacts.

Ask another Your Realty Link agent if you can host their listing's open house. You get the leads; they get the exposure. It's the fastest way to start with no listings of your own.

03The sign-in ask

Most visitors will sign in if you ask with confidence. Make it normal.

Sign-in + qualifying script

You: Welcome in! Go ahead and sign in for me — the sellers like to know who visited, and I'll send you the info sheet and any price updates.

Are you working with an agent yet, or just starting to look? (listen)

How long have you been looking? And is there a timeline you're hoping to move by?

You: If this one isn't the one, I've got a few others that might fit — want me to send them over?

04The follow-up that actually converts

The money is in the follow-up. Most agents collect sign-ins and never call. You will reach out within 24 hours — that alone puts you ahead of the field.
