YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 3.3: Writing Offers

01Price it with comps, not emotion

Your buyer needs to know what the home is really worth. Pull recent comparable sales, factor in condition and market heat, and recommend a number backed by data — then let the buyer decide.

In a hot segment, the right offer may be at or above list with strong terms. In a slow one, there's room to negotiate. Know which you're in before you write.

02Terms beyond price

These often matter as much as the number. Know your buyer's flexibility on each.

03The purchase agreement

In Indiana you'll write the offer on the standard Purchase Agreement plus the appropriate addenda. Fill it out completely and accurately — sloppy paperwork kills deals and trust.

Use the current Indiana/IAR Purchase Agreement and required addenda (including any buyer-broker compensation addendum). Confirm the exact forms and process with Janet, and prepare documents in Dotloop.
