01Prospecting, demystified

Prospecting has a scary reputation, but it's just conversations: your sphere, open-house visitors, past clients, new people you meet. The winners aren't the smoothest talkers — they're the ones who do it consistently during a protected block.

Pair this with your lead-gen block from Module 1.4. Same time, every day, before the world gets loud.

02Your daily prospecting menu

Pick the mix you'll actually do. Consistency beats intensity.

03A simple pipeline

Every lead is in one of these stages. Know who's where and what's next.

New → Nurturing → Active (appointment set) → Under contract → Closed. Your only job each day is to move people to the next stage. A lead with no next step is a lead you'll lose.

04Handle the 'no' without flinching

Objection: 'We're not ready yet'

You: Totally understand — most people aren't ready the day they start thinking about it.

Would it be helpful if I just kept you posted on what's happening with prices in your area, no pressure? That way when you are ready, you're informed.

(If yes → add to database + monthly touch plan.)
