YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 2.1: Your Economic Model

01Work backward, not forward

Most agents run the model forward: work hard, see what happens, hope it's enough. The model runs better in reverse. Net income you want → gross commission you must produce → closed sides → listings and buyers → appointments → leads. Each step is division.

Once you've done it, you stop asking “am I doing enough?” and start asking “did I hit twenty-six appointments this month?” — a question that has an answer.

Enter your numbers, not benchmarks. Pull your average sale price and your average commission per side from your own last twelve months of closings, or ask Janet Giles-Schultz (317-997-7404) to run them with you.
