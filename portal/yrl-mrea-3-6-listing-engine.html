01The second job

A properly marketed listing should generate about one closed buyer on its own. Sign calls, open house visitors, flyer-box inquiries, mailer responses — all of it is lead generation you have already paid for.

This is also why listings beat buyers economically. They take less of your time per dollar, they give you control of your schedule, and they advertise you to everyone who drives past. Buyers, by comparison, generate almost no marketing at all.

The failure mode is a hot market. The house sells in four days, everyone celebrates, and nobody captured a single lead from it. Your checklist has to survive your own get-it-sold instincts — that's what a checklist is for.
