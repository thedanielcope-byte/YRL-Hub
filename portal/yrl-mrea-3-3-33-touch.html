01What 33 touches buys you

Worked properly, every 12 people in your Met database produce about 2 closings a year — one repeat, one referral. That ratio is the engine of a referral-based business, and it only holds at genuine frequency.

Frequency beats creativity here, and it isn't close. Agents burn months designing the perfect piece when the stronger correlation is simply with how consistently and how often the message arrives.

Every touch — all thirty-three — carries a reminder that you work by referral and instructions on how to send you one. That is the mechanism. A touch that doesn't ask is a greeting card.
