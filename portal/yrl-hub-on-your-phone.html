Hub

Put the YRL Hub on your phone

Add yrlagent.com to your home screen so the hub opens with one tap, like an app.

Watch on YouTube

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open yrlagent.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow). It's at the bottom on iPhone and the top right on iPad.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name "YRL Hub" and tap Add.
  5. Tap the red YRL icon on your home screen to open the hub.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open yrlagent.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.
  4. Tap the YRL Hub icon to open the hub.
Already added it before and see a different icon? Delete it from your home screen and add it again.
