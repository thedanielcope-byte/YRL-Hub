YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 6.4: New Construction

01Why buyers need you in new construction

The friendly person in the model home works for the builder, not the buyer. Buyers who bring their own agent get an advocate on price, upgrades, timelines, and the builder's contract — which is written to protect the builder.

Register as the buyer's agent on the very first visit — many builders require your presence at first contact for you to be paid. The builder typically pays the buyer-agent compensation, but confirm it in writing on each deal; don't assume.
