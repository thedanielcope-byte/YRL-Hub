YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 3.4: Negotiating Win-Win Agreements

01The negotiator's mindset

Both sides want the deal to close. Your job is to advocate hard for your client and keep the other side willing to say yes. Stay calm, stay factual, and never let emotion — theirs or yours — blow up a good deal.

The best negotiators listen more than they talk. Understand what the other side actually needs, and you'll find the trade that works.

02Counteroffers

Presenting a counter to your buyer

You: They came back at [X]. Here's what the comps support and here's what matters most to you. I'd suggest we counter at [Y] and hold firm on [key term].

This keeps us in a strong position without losing the house. How do you feel about that?

03Inspection & repair negotiation

After inspection, focus on what matters — safety, systems, and big-ticket items — not every cosmetic nit. Ask for repairs, a credit, or a price reduction, and know your buyer's walk-away line before you respond.

04Appraisal gaps & curveballs

If a home appraises below the offer, you have levers: renegotiate price, the buyer covers the gap, or you challenge the appraisal with better comps. Know these before they happen so you're the calm expert in the room.
