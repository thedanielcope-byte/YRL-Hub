YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 4.2: Your Prelisting Packet

01Why send it before you meet

When a seller reviews who you are and how you sell homes before your appointment, you show up as the expert they've already chosen. It also lets you spend the meeting listening and pricing, not selling from scratch.

Read the YRL Pre-Listing Presentation over and over until you know it cold — you should be able to speak to every point without the packet in front of you.

02What to send ahead

Find the current Pre-Listing Presentation and forms in the YRL agent shared docs (SharePoint — YRL Agents). Confirm the latest versions and the buyer-broker-compensation talking points with Janet before you send.

03Do your homework before the meeting

Walk in prepared — it's how you earn instant credibility.
