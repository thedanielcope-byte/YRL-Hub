YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 2.5: Red Light, Green Light

01The rule

Add an expense. Then require that expense to produce a matching increase in results before you add the next one. Red light. You sit at that light until the result shows up. When it does, the light turns green and you may spend again.

That is the entire method, and it does something subtle: it converts risk into information. You are never guessing whether marketing works — you are running a sequence of small, cheap, answerable tests.

The failure mode is “cost creep” — expenses that rise steadily with no matching rise in results. It is rarely one bad decision. It is twelve small ones nobody ever went back and checked.
