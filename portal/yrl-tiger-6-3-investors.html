YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 6.3: Working with Investors

01How investors think

Investors don't buy on emotion — they buy on numbers. Cash flow, cap rate, repair costs, and return on investment drive their decisions. Speak their language and bring them real deals, and they'll keep coming back.

02Know the fundamentals

You don't need to be an appraiser — but you do need to run numbers credibly. Partner with lenders, contractors, and property managers you trust.

03Find & serve investors

Investors are in your sphere, at REIA meetings, and among landlords in your farm. Bring them deals that pencil out — including off-market opportunities — and become their go-to.
