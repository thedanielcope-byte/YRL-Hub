01Your license

You'll complete Indiana's required pre-licensing education, pass the state exam, and activate your license under Your Realty Link. Keep your license number handy — you'll need it to be set up in our systems.

Confirm the current pre-licensing provider, exam steps, and how to activate your license under Your Realty Link with Janet Giles-Schultz (317-997-7404). Steps and providers change — verify before you rely on them.

03Why MIBOR matters

MIBOR is our local REALTOR association and MLS. Membership gives you access to the BLC (our MLS), the forms and lockbox systems, and the professional standards you'll work under. You must be a MIBOR member to be the listing agent on a property.
