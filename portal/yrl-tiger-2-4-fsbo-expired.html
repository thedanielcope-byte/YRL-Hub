YOUR REALTY LINK · TIGER TRAINING — Module 2.4: FSBO & Expired Listings

01Why FSBOs and expireds

A FSBO wants to sell but is trying to avoid paying an agent. An expired listing wanted to sell but didn't — and is often frustrated. Both are motivated. Your job is to show up with genuine help, not a hard pitch.

Lead with empathy and value. These sellers get hammered by agents — be the one who's actually useful and different.

02The FSBO approach

You're not attacking their choice — you're offering help.

FSBO first-contact script

You: Hi, I saw your home for sale by owner — it looks great. I'm [Name] with Your Realty Link.

I'm not calling to pressure you to list. Selling on your own is a lot of work, so I wanted to offer a hand — a free market analysis so you know you're priced right, and I'm happy to bring you any buyers I'm working with.

Out of curiosity, if you found the right buyer through an agent, would you be open to working with one? (listen)

03The expired approach

They're frustrated. Acknowledge it, then show a better plan.

Expired first-contact script

You: Hi [Name], I noticed your home didn't sell and just came off the market — I imagine that's frustrating after all that effort.

I've studied why homes in your area sit, and I do things differently. Would you be open to a quick conversation about what it would take to actually get it sold this time?

(If open → set the appointment; bring a real pricing + marketing plan.)
