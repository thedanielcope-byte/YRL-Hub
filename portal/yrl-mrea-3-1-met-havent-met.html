YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 3.1: Met vs Haven't Met

01Two buckets, that's all

Met is anyone who would recognise your name — past clients, sphere, people you've spoken with on the phone, anyone you've actually met. They produce repeat and referral business, which is the cheapest business there is.

Haven't Met is a chosen audience that doesn't know you yet — a geographic farm, a neighbourhood, a demographic list. They produce new business. It costs more per sale, but it scales without consuming your hours.

A cold form-fill that never turned into a conversation is Haven't Met, no matter how it arrived. The test is simple: would they recognise your name? If not, they haven't met you.
