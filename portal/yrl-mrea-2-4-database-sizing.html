YOUR REALTY LINK · THE MILLIONAIRE AGENT PATH — Module 2.4: Database Sizing

01Two audiences, two ratios

Everyone you could do business with falls into one of two groups. Met — people who know you, because you've spoken or met in person. Haven't Met — a farm, a neighbourhood, a demographic list; people you've chosen to market to who don't know you yet.

Each behaves predictably when worked properly. For every 12 people in your Met database that you touch 33 times a year, expect about 2 closings — one repeat, one referral. For every 50 people in your Haven't Met database that you mail 12 times a year, expect about 1 closing.

Met business costs less money per sale but more of your personal time. Haven't Met costs more money but scales without your hours — which is why any agent who wants to eventually step back needs both.

04Feed it every day

There are about 240 working days in a year. Adding one name a day is 240 names — it sounds trivial and it compounds into a business. Adding three a day fills most agents' entire requirement inside of three years, from a standing start.

The four laws are short: build a database, feed it every day, communicate with it systematically, and service every lead that comes your way.
