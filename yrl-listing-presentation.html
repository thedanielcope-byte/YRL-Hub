Pricing Your Home
The right price attracts buyers. The wrong price costs you time and money.
Suggested List Price
Based on comparable sales and current market conditions

 Priced Right

  • More showings in the first two weeks
  • Potential for multiple offers
  • Buyers compete, driving price up
  • Your home sells — not sits

 Overpriced

  • Fewer showings from the start
  • Helps sell comparable homes instead
  • Days on market increase, buyers wonder why
  • Eventually sells for less than market value

We don't make the market — we interpret it. Our job is to position your home so it's one of the properties that actually sells, instead of one that sits on the market losing value day after day.
