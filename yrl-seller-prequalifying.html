Seller Pre-Qualifying Script

19 questions to ask before every listing appointment

Seller Tools

Before Every Listing Appointment

This call qualifies the seller, confirms the appointment, and gives you the information you need to prepare a winning presentation. Complete this call within 24 hours of the appointment.

Within 24 hours of appointment
No exceptions
Takes about 10 minutes

 Opening

Hi, this is with Your Realty Link. I'm calling to confirm our appointment on at . Does that time still work for you?
Great. Before I come out to your house, there are a couple of questions I need to ask you so I am fully prepared and you save time. Do you have 10 minutes right now?
Great. If what I say makes sense and you feel comfortable — and confident that I can sell your house — are you planning to list your house with me when I come out on at ?
1

Are you interviewing other agents?

Understand the competitive landscape

Do you plan on interviewing more than one agent for the job to sell your house? If yes, that's okay — note it and plan to address it in your presentation.
2

Where are you moving to?

Understand their motivation and timeline

Tell me again — where are you moving to?
3

What's your timeline?

Create urgency and understand constraints

How soon do you have to be there? If I sell your house in the next 30 days, would that pose a problem for you?
4

What if it doesn't sell?

Uncover the real motivation

What would happen if your house did NOT sell? This reveals how motivated they really are. Listen carefully.
5

What price do you have in mind?

Gauge expectations early

When I see you, how much do you want to list your house for realistically? As a professional real estate agent, I study houses and prices every day. So I'll assume you'll list with me at the price that will cause it to sell — correct?
6

What's your bottom line?

Understand their floor

So what's the price you won't go below?
7

How much do you owe?

Check for equity or payoff issues

How much do you owe on the property? This helps you calculate net proceeds and identify potential issues early.
8

Have you considered selling yourself?

Address FSBO mindset upfront

Have you ever thought about selling it yourself? If yes, this helps you prepare your value proposition for the listing appointment.
9

Owner financing or cash?

Understand financial needs

Will you help finance the house for the buyer, or do you need your cash out?
10

Buying another home?

Identify a potential buyer client too

Are you going to purchase another home? Do you need the money from this house to buy the next home? If yes, this is a buyer opportunity too. Make note of it.
11

Are you current on payments?

Check for financial distress

Are you current on your house payments? If they are behind, reassure them: "I work with many people in that situation and it's becoming more common. I'm experienced at working with lenders on these transactions, so you're in good hands."
12

Describe your house

Get familiar before you arrive

Will you quickly describe your house for me?
13

Rate your house 1–10

Get their self-assessment

How would you rate your house on a scale from 1 to 10?
14

What would make it a 10?

Identify known issues

What would make it a 10? This reveals what they know needs fixing or updating.
15

Anything that affects the price?

Uncover hidden positives or negatives

Is there anything positive or negative about your house that could affect the price?
16

Pre-listing package

Set expectations

I'll be sending over a package of information. Will you take a few moments and review it prior to my arrival?
17

Any questions before I arrive?

Open the floor

Do you have any questions before I arrive?
18

Other decision makers?

Make sure everyone is involved

I've only spoken to you. Are there any other decision makers I should speak with before I come over on ?
19

Will everyone be there?

Confirm all parties will attend

Will all the decision makers be there when I arrive?

 Wrap Up

So you know, our meeting will only take about 30 minutes. Will that be okay?
